AFNAN FATHIMA BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
==============================

Start page: index.html (the invitation). It leads to home.html, which links
to every chapter. Pages, in journey order:

  index.html        invitation card
  home.html         hero, poem, "the stars aligned", the five chapter doors
  timetravel.html   Chapter 1  Birthday chronicles (timeline)
  memories.html     Chapter 2  Bloom (stacking photo cards, her song)
  wishes.html       Chapter 3  Echoes of love (7 video wishes)
  gallery.html      Chapter 4  The vault (photos, films, screening room)
  cake.html         Chapter 5  Blow the candles and cut the 3D cake
  masterpiece.html  Final reveal with fireworks

All shared design and code live in:  assets/style.css  and  assets/app.js


PUTTING THE REAL PHOTOS BACK
----------------------------
Every photo is now a colorful placeholder that shows its own file name.
To use a real photo, copy it into the same folder with exactly the same
name and extension (for example media/p/p9.jpeg). Nothing else needs to
change. Tip: resize big photos to about 1600 px on the long side first
so the site stays fast on phones.

VIEWING AND SHARING
-------------------
Double-click index.html to open it. For the 3D cake and the videos to work
everywhere, upload the whole folder to a free static host such as
Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages or Vercel and share the link.
The site needs internet for its fonts and for the 3D cake library.
